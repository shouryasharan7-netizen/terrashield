# TERRASHIELD Data Attribution & Citations

1. **NASA FIRMS (Fire Information for Resource Management System)**
   - Provider: Earth Science Data and Information System (ESDIS) project, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center.
   - Sensor Data: Visible Infrared Imaging Radiometer Suite (VIIRS) on Suomi-NPP & NOAA-20; MODIS on Terra & Aqua.
   - Citation: NASA Near Real-Time Active Fire Data distributed by NASA FIRMS. https://earthdata.nasa.gov/firms

2. **Open-Meteo Weather & Air Quality Service**
   - Provider: Open-Meteo.com under Non-Commercial & Open Data License.
   - Sources: Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS), NOAA National Centers for Environmental Prediction (NCEP), DWD ICON Model.
   - Citation: Zippenfenig, P. (2023). Open-Meteo Weather API. https://open-meteo.com

3. **OpenAQ Community Data**
   - Provider: OpenAQ Global Open Air Quality Initiative.
   - Coverage: Government-monitored ground air monitoring stations globally.
   - Citation: OpenAQ Community (2024). Global Ambient Air Quality Data. https://openaq.org

4. **Global Forest Watch**
   - Provider: World Resources Institute (WRI) in collaboration with University of Maryland (GLAD) and Google.
   - Data: Tree cover loss alerts (Sentinel-2 and Landsat optical/SAR data).
   - Citation: Hansen, M. C. et al. Global Forest Watch. https://www.globalforestwatch.org
